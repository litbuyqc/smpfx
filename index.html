Oopbuy shopping guide

From an interesting find to an informed choice.

A useful Oopbuy finds list should help you compare products. Keep the original listing URL and record what you have checked, what remains uncertain and when you last reviewed it.

  1. Choose one category first

    Start with a specific need: an everyday sneaker, a layering piece or an accessory. Compare similar products together.

  2. Read beyond the main photo

    Check the selected color, material description, measurements and included items. A styled photo may show pieces sold separately.

  3. Record your comparison

    Save the product URL, selected variant, listed price, size notes and unresolved questions in your own spreadsheet.

  4. Verify the purchase route

    Confirm seller details, current availability, payment options, delivery costs and return terms. Check whether your chosen service accepts the listing.

What belongs in your shortlist?

Keep measurable details beside every link so that your decisions remain easy to review.

Suggested checks for a personal product comparison spreadsheet
Product group Record these details Resolve before ordering
Shoes Model, color, size system, size chart and available foot-length guidance. Is the sizing information specific to the selected model and variant?
Hoodies & T-shirts Chest width, garment length, shoulders, sleeves and stated fabric. Are the measurements for the garment or the wearer's body?
Jackets & sets Layering room, closure details, lining and the pieces included. Does the listing sell the full set or an individual piece?
Pants & shorts Waist, rise, inseam, leg opening and adjustment options. Are the waist measurements relaxed, stretched or adjustable?
Headwear & accessories Dimensions, materials, closures and available adjustment range. Do the dimensions and fastening suit your intended use?
Jerseys & underwear Fit, fabric, personalization options and return restrictions. Are customized or hygiene-sensitive items eligible for returns?
Compare the full expected cost: item price + domestic delivery + any service charges + international shipping + applicable taxes or duties. Check currency conversion costs separately. A product's displayed price may cover only the item.
Clear answers

Before you follow a product link.

Understand what this guide provides and which details need checking with the seller or service you use.

What is an Oopbuy spreadsheet?

An Oopbuy spreadsheet is a shared product list used to organize shopping links and comparison notes. Its contents depend on its creator; inclusion does not establish product quality, authenticity or current availability.

Is this the official Oopbuy website?

No. This is an independent discovery guide with links to CAFANS categories. It is not operated by or affiliated with Oopbuy or the fashion brands shown.

Can I download a spreadsheet here?

No downloadable spreadsheet is provided on this page. Use the category links to browse CAFANS and keep your own shortlist of product URLs, sizes, prices and comparison notes.

How should I compare clothing sizes?

Compare the listing's measurements with a garment you already own. Check the measurement method and units, and consider chest width, length, shoulders and sleeves rather than relying only on a size label.

Does a product photo prove authenticity?

No. Photos can help compare visible details, but they do not prove authenticity. Check the seller, product provenance and applicable purchase protections before ordering.

What costs should I check before buying?

Check the item price, domestic delivery, any service charges, international shipping, currency conversion costs and applicable taxes or duties. Verify the current amounts and policies with the services involved.
